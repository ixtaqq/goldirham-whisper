Goldirham Whisper

A reference calendar of exchange operations dates and second-tier statistical releases. Each entry is an all-day date in the issuer's home time zone.

Compiled every six hours from public exchange notices, statistical release calendars and central-bank feeds, plus entries added by hand. Issuers move and cancel dates, so check the source notice.
